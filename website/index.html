A research prototype of an evidence vault for women facing online harassment in Bangladesh. It keeps screenshots, photos and notes encrypted on the phone and drafts a General Diary (GD) text for manual filing. Not for real use: unsafe for survivors until reviewed by a security expert and a field partner.

See How it Works Under the Hood

Chaos The Reality

About 64% of the 359 women who completed a 2022 ActionAid Bangladesh online survey said they had faced online violence (Prothom Alo, 28 Nov 2022; not a national sample). Stigma keeps many from reporting.

  • ✗ Evidence Loss: Victims often delete messages out of fear, destroying crucial proof.
  • ✗ Legal Complexity: Filing a General Diary (GD) and working out which cyber law applies is intimidating.
  • ✗ Metadata Gap: Screenshots without dates, links and account names are easy to doubt.

Clarity The Protiti Solution

We help survivors keep their evidence in one safe, organised place.

  • ✓ On-device encryption: Evidence is stored in an encrypted SQLCipher database on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • ✓ GD draft: A guided form makes a PDF draft to file in person. There is no e-GD integration, and no lawyer has reviewed the output.
  • ✓ Works offline: The vault, the GD draft and the SMS SOS do not need mobile data.

The Architecture of Safety

📦

Secure Evidence Vault

Encrypted SQLCipher storage on the phone. A separate duress PIN opens a decoy vault for coercion scenarios.

📝

GD Draft Wizard

A guided form turns your account of the incident and your evidence list into a GD draft PDF. You file it yourself; there is no police API.

🚨

Panic Screen

Sends one SMS with your location to your saved contacts, and dials 999, 109 or the PCSW hotline with one tap. It does not alert PCSW by itself.

🤝

Support Directory

A list of hotlines, legal aid and counselling services. No organisation has agreed to a partnership yet.

Target Community & Impact

Intended for female university students (18-30) facing digital harassment. The 8-month plan includes campus workshops and a pilot; no pilot has run yet. The goals below are aims, not results.

Keep

Fewer survivors lose evidence by deleting it in panic.

Draft

More complete GD drafts that a duty officer can act on.

Refer

Faster contact with PCSW, the police unit for women facing cyber harassment, in extortion and deepfake cases.

The Evidence (Amendment) Act 2022 lets courts in Bangladesh consider digital records. Protiti aims to help survivors keep those records safe and organised. No lawyer has yet reviewed whether its output would be accepted in court.

Every woman deserves a frame of safety. Help us build the structure for justice.
