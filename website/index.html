A Digital Evidence Vault app designed for women facing online harassment in Bangladesh. Protiti provides a secure, encrypted framework to document screenshots, links, and timestamps of abuse in a format that is legally admissible and ready for police reporting.

See How it Works

Chaos The Reality

64% of Bangladeshi women face digital violence. Victims are left vulnerable without structure.

  • ✗ Evidence Loss: Victims delete messages/accounts out of fear, destroying crucial proof.
  • ✗ Legal Complexity: General Diary (GD) filing is intimidating and confusing.
  • ✗ Metadata Gap: Simple screenshots are easily dismissed. They lack timestamps and source URLs.

Clarity The Solution

Protiti provides a frame of safety to structure the path to justice.

  • ✓ Zero-Knowledge Security: End-to-end encryption with local SQLCipher on-device storage.
  • ✓ Legal Logic Engine: Mapped directly to the Cyber Protection Act, 2026.
  • ✓ Resilience: Offline-first architecture ensures the vault works without active mobile internet.

Core Features

📦

Secure Evidence Vault

Biometric-locked, encrypted storage with a Duress PIN (Decoy Vault) for physical coercion scenarios.

📝

GD-Automator

Maps data to the Cyber Protection Act 2026 and designed for direct Bangladesh Police e-GD API integration.

🚨

Panic Frame

Offline-first SOS alert with lightweight SMS coordinates for low-connectivity environments.

🤝

Support Bridge

Curated directory of pro-bono lawyers and mental health counselors for victims to find safe help.

The User Journey

1

Capture

Victim captures evidence (screenshot, link, text).

2

Structure

Protiti auto-tags metadata (time, date, source, cryptographic hash).

3

Analyze

User answers guided questions about the incident in a safe, trauma-informed interface.

4

Formulate & Action

App generates a structured legal complaint (GD format). Export securely to police or lawyer.
