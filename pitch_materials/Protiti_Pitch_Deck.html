THE REALITY: About 64% of the 359 women who completed a 2022 ActionAid Bangladesh online survey said they had faced online violence (Prothom Alo, 28 Nov 2022; not a national sample).
SLIDE 02 • THE REALITY
The Problem: Online Violence Destroys Evidence & Silences Victims
Trauma, legal complexity, and physical coercion create an insurmountable barrier to justice.
🚨 1. Panic Deletion
Out of fear, or worry that family will find out, many victims delete chats and block abusers, destroying proof before police can act.
Result: With no evidence left, police have little to act on.
⚖️ 2. Legal Intimidation
Cyber law in Bangladesh has changed more than once since 2023. Drafting a General Diary (GD) is bewildering for traumatised victims.
Result: Unstructured complaints are hard for duty officers to act on.
🔍 3. The Forensic Gap
Screenshots without links, dates and account names are easy to doubt. The Evidence (Amendment) Act 2022 allows digital records, with forensic examination if authenticity is disputed.
Result: Poorly kept evidence can weaken a genuine complaint.
📱 4. Physical Coercion
Abusive partners or family members frequently demand phones. An obvious anti-harassment app invites immediate domestic escalation and physical assault.
Result: Survivors are forced to delete safety tools or face violence.
SLIDE 03 • THE SOLUTION
The Solution: Protiti (প্রতীতি) — Structural Safety & Conviction
An encrypted, trauma-informed digital assistant engineered specifically for Bangladesh.
🔒 On-Device Secure Vault (SQLCipher)
• On-device only: Evidence is encrypted on the phone (SQLCipher, AES-256). Nothing is uploaded.
• Duress PIN (Decoy Vault): A second PIN, chosen by the user, opens a separate decoy vault.
• Stealth Calculator Disguise: Functional arithmetic utility masks the vault.
📝 GD Draft
• Guided form: Short questions turn the survivor's account and evidence list into a GD draft.
• Manual filing: No e-GD integration and no automatic law mapping. A lawyer or PCSW officer should check the draft.
• PDF output: Printable PDF. Bangla text in the PDF is a known bug.
🚨 Offline Panic SOS Frame
• 3-Second Hold Trigger: Radial progress circle with haptics prevents accidental triggers.
• Zero-Data SMS Fallback: Dispatches GPS coordinates via cellular SMS without mobile internet.
• Direct Emergency Hotlines: One-tap dialing for 999, 109 and the PCSW hotline (01320000888).
🤝 Support Directory
• Legal aid (proposed): Referral routes to BLAST and BNWLA are proposed. Neither has agreed.
• Crisis Counseling: Integrated directory for Kaan Pete Roi & Moner Janala.
• PCSW: Contact details for the police unit for women facing cyber harassment (to verify with PCSW).
SLIDE 04 • USER JOURNEY
How It Works: 5-Stage Journey From Chaos to Certitude
A trauma-informed path from harassment to an organised GD draft.
1. Capture
Survivor saves a screenshot, photo, voice note or written note before blocking the abuser.
2. Authenticate
Protiti records the phone's date and time and a SHA-256 file hash. This is not a forensic timestamp.
3. Triage
Trauma-informed 4-step wizard gathers incident facts in plain, supportive Bangla.
4. Formulate
The app lays the facts out as a General Diary draft PDF. It does not pick legal sections.
5. Action
Survivor files the draft at the thana or shares it with a lawyer or PCSW.
BEFORE vs AFTER PROTITI:
BEFORE: Panic deletion → 0 legal proof → Duty officer rejection → Abuser impunity.
AFTER: Encrypted vault on the phone → Organised evidence list → GD draft ready to file → A clearer case for police.
SLIDE 05 • ARCHITECTURE
Technology & Security: What the Prototype Does
Not yet reviewed by a security expert or tested on a real phone.
🔐 Encrypted Vault & Duress PIN
• SQLCipher AES-256: Cryptographic keys stored inside Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
• Two PINs: The real PIN (6 or more digits, set at first launch) opens the vault; a separate duress PIN opens a decoy vault. No default PINs.
• Separate files: Real and decoy vaults use separate database files and keys. We do not claim the real vault is hidden from a forensic search.
📲 Calculator Disguise
• Working calculator: The app opens as a calculator. The home-screen icon is not disguised yet.
• Stealth unlock: Typing your PIN followed by = opens the vault.
• Wipe code: A wipe code that the user chooses triggers the vault wipe.
📡 Zero-Data SMS Fallback
• Offline Survivability: Compact GPS coordinates (<160 chars) transmitted over standard cellular SMS.
• No internet needed: Works during data outages. It still needs mobile signal and SMS credit.
• Hotlines: One tap dials 999, 109 or the PCSW hotline.
⚖️ Evidence Notes
• SHA-256 hashes: Recorded for each stored file. Today the app hashes the encrypted file, not the original.
• No certificate: The app does not produce a certificate of authenticity. Admissibility needs review by counsel.
• Device clock: Times come from the phone's clock, so they are not independent proof.
