# Slide 2: The Problem (Chaos)
*Online violence creates chaos. Victims are left vulnerable without structure.*

- **Evidence Loss:** Victims delete messages/accounts out of fear, destroying crucial proof.
- **Legal Complexity:** General Diary (GD) filing and navigating the Cyber Security Act are intimidating and confusing.
- **Metadata Gap:** Simple screenshots are easily dismissed. They lack timestamps, URLs, and source metadata that courts require.
- **Isolation:** Victims often don't know where to find trusted legal or mental health support.
- **Context:** In Bangladesh, social stigma and the digital divide amplify these issues, silencing victims before they can seek help.

*Visual Suggestion:* A collage of blurred, overlapping chat bubbles, legal documents, and red exclamation marks representing the overwhelming chaos and confusion faced by victims.

---

# Slide 3: The Solution — Shield-Frame (Structure)
*Providing a frame of safety to structure the path to justice.*

- **Secure Evidence Vault:** Cloud-based, biometric-locked storage with auto-metadata extraction.
- **GD-Automator:** Simple Q&A interface that translates user inputs into a professionally drafted complaint in both Bengali and English.
- **Panic Frame:** One-touch emergency alert that shares location and evidence vault access with trusted contacts.
- **Support Bridge:** A curated network connecting victims with vetted pro-bono lawyers and mental health counselors.

*Visual Suggestion:* A clean, organized dashboard UI mockup showing the four main features neatly aligned within a structural "frame," emphasizing clarity and control.

# Slide 4: How It Works (User Journey)
*From Chaos to Clarity.*

1. **Capture:** Victim captures evidence (screenshot, link, text).
2. **Structure:** Shield-Frame auto-tags metadata (time, date, source, cryptographic hash).
3. **Analyze:** User answers guided questions about the incident in a safe, trauma-informed interface.
4. **Formulate:** App generates a structured legal complaint (GD format).
5. **Action:** Export securely to police, lawyer, or a Support Bridge contact.

*Visual Suggestion:* A flowchart or a "Before & After" graphic. Left side: "Messy Chat" with erratic lines. Right side: Shield-Frame processing it into a "Clean Legal Complaint" with straight, organized lines.

# Slide 5: Technology & Innovation
*Built for security. Designed for survival.*

- **Zero-Knowledge Security:** End-to-end encryption (even developers cannot see the evidence).
- **Disguise Mode:** App appears as a harmless utility (e.g., calculator or notes app) to protect victims from abusers with device access.
- **Legal Logic Engine:** Algorithm mapped to the Bangladesh Cyber Security Act to suggest relevant legal pathways based on evidence.
- **Localized Support:** Division-specific police station and help desk finder via GPS.
- **Resilience:** Offline-capable architecture ensures the vault and GD generation work without an internet connection.

*Visual Suggestion:* An isometric view of a smartphone. A padlock icon locking the screen, with binary code or a schematic overlay demonstrating the underlying robust architecture.
