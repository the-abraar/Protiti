# Design System & Brand Handoff: Protiti (প্রতীতি)

## 1. Brand Naming & Strategy
Following a comprehensive brand uniqueness audit across Bangladesh civic tech, UNDP project registries, and Google Play:

### Official Brand Name: **Protiti (প্রতীতি)**
* **Bengali:** প্রতীতি
* **Phonetics:** */pro-t̪i-t̪i/ (Pro-tee-tee)*
* **Linguistic Root & Meaning:** Derived from classical Bengali epistemology. *Protiti* signifies **"Deep Conviction," "Undisputed Certitude,"** and **"The Realization of Truth through Valid Proof."** It captures the decisive moment when forensic evidence dissolves doubt and truth becomes undeniable.
* **Tagline:**
  * *Bangla:* **"সন্দেহহীন সত্য, অকাট্য প্রমাণের অধিকার।"**
  * *English:* **"From Doubt to Certitude: Structure for Digital Justice"**

### Why Earlier Candidates Were Dropped:
1. **Aparajita (অপরাজিতা):** Heavily saturated across UNDP Bangladesh (*Aparajita: Women's Political Empowerment*), BRAC, and microfinance NGOs. Tipped off abusers due to universal GBV association.
2. **Abhaya (অভয়া):** Already in active use by multiple apps and initiatives (e.g., Asansol-Durgapur Police 'Abhaya' app, post-2024 Kolkata protest memorials, 'Abhaya Well').
3. **Shield-Frame (Working Code Name):** Replaced as too technical/militaristic for survivor-facing engagement.

### Uniqueness & Strategic Fit:
* **100% Collision-Free:** Verified zero mobile applications or civic-tech initiatives with this name.
* **Youth Cultural Resonance:** Deep cultural bond with Bangladeshi youth (ages 18–35) via the iconic Artcell track *"Protiti"*, making it culturally aspirational rather than bureaucratic.
* **Launcher Stealth (9/10):** Blends naturally on a phone home screen as a reflective reader or journal app, protecting user privacy.

## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Language

### Core Attributes
*Empowering, Secure, Professional, Calm, and Trustworthy.*

### Color Palette
To move away from stereotypical or overly aggressive "security" colors, we are adopting a palette that balances strength with calm professionalism:

* **Primary (Strength & Dignity):** Deep Amethyst `#4A154B`
* **Secondary (Growth & Clarity):** Teal `#008080`
* **Danger/Alert (Urgency):** Crimson Red `#D32F2F`
* **Accent (Hope & Focus):** Warm Gold `#FFC107`
* **Surface/Background (Light Mode):** Alabaster `#F8F9FA`
* **Surface/Background (Dark Mode):** Soft Charcoal `#121212` with `#1E1E1E` for elevated cards.

### Typography
The app will heavily rely on text readability for legal documents and evidence viewing, requiring a clean, modern, and highly legible bilingual type system.

* **Primary Typeface (English):** *Poppins* - Geometric, modern, friendly but professional. Used for headings and primary UI elements.
* **Secondary/Body Typeface (English):** *Inter* - Maximum readability for long-form text and evidence details.
* **Bengali Typeface:** *Hind Siliguri* or *Noto Sans Bengali* - Excellent legibility for bilingual UI support and General Diary (GD) generation.

### Logo Concept: The Shield & The Bloom
**Concept:** A minimalist, elegant icon that combines the structural security of a shield with the organic, resilient form of a blooming lotus (representing rising above adversity). 
**Execution:** Clean line art. Not aggressive or overly militaristic. The logo should feel like a trusted companion rather than a weapon. It should also be adaptable to the "App Disguise Mode" (e.g., transforming into a generic calculator icon seamlessly).

---

## 3. UI/UX Principles
1. **Trauma-Informed Design:** Avoid harsh, triggering language or jarring animations. Use calm transitions and clear, supportive microcopy.
2. **Speed & Accessibility:** The "Panic Frame" must be accessible within 1 second of opening the app. High contrast for readability.
3. **Discreet Operation:** The UI should have a quick-exit gesture (e.g., swipe down to instantly close or disguise the app).
