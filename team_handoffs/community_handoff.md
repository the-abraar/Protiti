# DKC Fellowship Project: Community Action Plan & Go-To-Market Strategy

**Duration:** 2 Days

*   **09:00 AM - 10:00 AM:** Welcome & Introduction to the DKC Project Vision.

## 2. Strategic Partnerships
To ensure the app connects users with real-world help and legal backing, we will partner with the following established organizations in Bangladesh:

1.  **Police Cyber Support for Women (PCSW):**
    *   *Role:* Official law enforcement integration. We will build a direct reporting API or dedicated escalation channel for severe cases requiring immediate police intervention.
2.  **Bangladesh National Woman Lawyer's Association (BNWLA):**
    *   *Role:* Legal aid and counsel. Partnering with BNWLA will allow us to offer users pro-bono legal advice and representation for cases of online harassment and exploitation.
3.  **Cyber Crime Awareness Foundation (CCA Foundation):**
    *   *Role:* Knowledge partner. They will assist in keeping our educational modules up-to-date with the latest cyber crime trends in Bangladesh and co-host awareness seminars.
4.  **BRAC (Gender Justice & Diversity Programme):**
    *   *Role:* Grassroots outreach. Leveraging BRAC’s extensive nationwide network to reach women in peri-urban and rural areas who might not be accessible via standard digital marketing.

## 3. Go-To-Market (GTM) & Community Outreach Strategy

**Phase 1: Foundation & Pilot (Month 1-2)**
*   **App Beta Testing:** Closed beta testing with 500 selected female university students.
*   **Ambassador Onboarding:** Conduct the first "Train the Trainer" workshops at 3 major universities in Dhaka (e.g., DU, NSU, BRACU).
*   **Partnership Formalization:** Sign MOUs with BNWLA and PCSW to finalize the escalation protocols.

**Phase 2: Digital Campaign & Campus Activation (Month 3-4)**
*   **Social Media Blitz:** Launch localized awareness campaigns on Facebook and TikTok focusing on digital rights and app utility, using relatable, culturally contextual storytelling.
*   **Campus Kiosks:** Set up activation booths in university campuses where Ambassadors assist in app downloads and basic safety checks.
*   **Webinars:** Host weekly live Q&A sessions with cybersecurity experts and lawyers.

**Phase 3: National Scale & Grassroots Penetration (Month 5+)**
*   **NGO Integration:** Begin joint outreach programs with BRAC across district levels.
*   **PR & Media:** Press releases in leading national dailies (Prothom Alo, Daily Star) and appearances on local morning TV talk shows.
*   **Community Forums:** Activate the in-app community forum for peer-to-peer support, moderated by top-performing Ambassadors.

---

## 4. Rollout Schedule (8 Divisions)

The rollout will be phased to ensure our support infrastructure (moderators, legal aid partners) scales appropriately with the user base.

*   **Month 1 - 2: Dhaka Division (Pilot)**
    *   Focus on major universities and dense urban areas. Perfecting the app based on high-volume feedback.
*   **Month 3: Chattogram & Sylhet Divisions**
    *   Expanding to the next major urban hubs. Onboarding regional language moderators (e.g., Sylheti dialects for community forums).
*   **Month 4: Rajshahi & Khulna Divisions**
    *   Targeting universities (e.g., RU, KU) and partnering with local women's organizations.
*   **Month 5: Barishal & Rangpur Divisions**
    *   Shifting focus toward NGO-assisted grassroots outreach, emphasizing digital literacy alongside app usage.
*   **Month 6: Mymensingh Division & National Consolidation**
    *   Completing the nationwide rollout. Hosting the first "National Digital Safety Summit" for women to celebrate milestones and recognize top community ambassadors.
