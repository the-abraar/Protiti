# 🎥 Shield-Frame (Aparajita) — Official Demo Script

*Use this script while screen-recording the Flutter app or HTML mockup. It is specifically designed to preemptively answer the judges' hardest questions.*

---

## 🎬 Introduction (0:00 - 0:20)
**Action:** Show the Lock Screen (Calculator Disguise).
**Script:** 
> "Hello judges. In Bangladesh, 64% of women face digital violence, but most evidence is lost because victims are forced to delete it. Meet **Aparajita** (formerly Shield-Frame), our Digital Evidence Vault. At first glance, it looks like a standard calculator—essential for victims whose abusers have access to their devices."

## 🔓 The Vault & Duress PIN (0:20 - 0:45)
**Action:** Type the real PIN/Biometric to enter the Vault. Point to the evidence grid.
**Script:** 
> "By entering a secret code, the true app unlocks. But we built for the worst-case scenario. If an abuser physically forces the victim to unlock the app, she can enter a **Duress PIN**. This secondary PIN opens a completely fake, empty vault, protecting her from further violence. Inside the real vault, all evidence is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher and AES-256."

## 📝 GD-Automator & Police Integration (0:45 - 1:15)
**Action:** Tap the 'Report' tab. Click through the stepper wizard.
**Script:** 
> "When the user is ready to report, they don't have to navigate complex legal jargon. Our GD-Automator asks simple questions and maps the incident directly to the new **Cyber Protection Act 2026**. Rather than just printing a PDF that a local officer might reject, our data architecture is designed to integrate directly with the Bangladesh Police's official **e-GD API**, bridging the gap between victim and law enforcement."

## 🚨 Offline-First Panic Button (1:15 - 1:40)
**Action:** Tap the 'Panic' tab. Show the pulsating SOS button.
**Script:** 
> "In an emergency, internet access is never guaranteed. Our Panic Frame is **Offline-First**. If data is cut off, pressing this button bypasses the internet entirely, falling back to a lightweight SMS payload containing the victim's GPS coordinates and a distress code to their trusted contacts and local NGOs like BNWLA."

## 🤝 Conclusion (1:40 - 2:00)
**Action:** Tap the 'Support' tab showing the local directory.
**Script:** 
> "Finally, the Support Bridge connects victims to vetted pro-bono lawyers and counselors in their specific division. We are giving victims Structure for Justice, and Space for Safety. Thank you."
