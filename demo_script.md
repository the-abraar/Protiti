# 🎥 Protiti (প্রতীতি) — Official Demo Script

*Use this script while screen-recording the Flutter app or HTML mockup. Record on a test phone with test data only: the app is a research prototype and not for real use until a security expert and a field partner have reviewed it.*

---

## 🎬 Introduction (0:00 - 0:20)
**Action:** Show the Lock Screen (Calculator Disguise).
**Script:** 
> "Hello judges. In a 2022 ActionAid Bangladesh online survey, about 64% of the 359 women who answered in full said they had faced online violence. Many delete the evidence out of fear. Meet **Protiti (প্রতীতি)**, our Digital Evidence Vault. At first glance, it looks like a standard calculator—essential for victims whose abusers have access to their devices."

## 🔓 The Vault & Duress PIN (0:20 - 0:45)
**Action:** Type the real PIN/Biometric to enter the Vault. Point to the evidence grid.
**Script:** 
> "By entering a secret code, the true app unlocks. But we built for the worst-case scenario. If an abuser physically forces the victim to unlock the app, she can enter a **Duress PIN**. This secondary PIN opens a separate decoy vault instead of the real one. Inside the real vault, all evidence is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher and AES-256, and nothing is uploaded."

## 📝 GD Draft (0:45 - 1:15)
**Action:** Tap the 'Report' tab. Click through the stepper wizard.
**Script:** 
> "When the user is ready to report, a short set of questions turns her account and her evidence list into a General Diary draft. She can take the PDF to the thana or share it with a lawyer. It does not pick legal sections for her and it does not connect to any police system; a lawyer or PCSW officer should check it."

## 🚨 Offline-First Panic Button (1:15 - 1:40)
**Action:** Tap the 'Panic' tab. Show the pulsating SOS button.
**Script:** 
> "In an emergency, internet access is never guaranteed. Our Panic Frame is **Offline-First**. If data is cut off, pressing this button bypasses the internet entirely, sending one SMS with her location to the contacts she has saved. One tap also dials 999, the 109 helpline or the PCSW hotline."

## 🤝 Conclusion (1:40 - 2:00)
**Action:** Tap the 'Support' tab showing the local directory.
**Script:** 
> "Finally, the support directory lists hotlines, legal aid and counselling services. Partnerships with legal aid groups are proposed, not yet agreed. We are giving victims Structure for Justice, and Space for Safety. Thank you."


---

*Source for the 64% figure: ActionAid Bangladesh, "Violence against women online in Bangladesh" (online survey, November 2022, 359 complete responses), reported by [Prothom Alo, 28 Nov 2022](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/15il1p6qqq). It is not a national sample.*
