# 20 Logo Concepts for Protiti (প্রতীতি)

These concepts are tailored for Protiti's dual-persona strategy: combining a trauma-informed default state (Wellness/Journal) with a hard-stealth toggled state (Math/Utility). All concepts utilize the core brand palette: Deep Amethyst (#4A154B), Teal (#008080), and Warm Gold (#FFC107).

## Category 1: Default Persona (Wellness & Journaling)
*These act as the primary, default app icons. They provide a sense of calm and safety while remaining completely innocuous to an observer.*

1. **The Secured Ribbon Journal:** An elegant closed journal book in Deep Amethyst. The Warm Gold bookmark ribbon elegantly loops into the subtle shape of a padlock's shackle at the bottom.
2. **The Protective Embrace:** Soft Teal and Warm Gold lotus petals or leaves wrapping around each other in a comforting embrace, subtly forming the outer silhouette of a soft, rounded shield.
3. **The Unfolding Truth:** A stylized, open journal where the pages gracefully flip upward and morph into a glowing lotus flower, with a single gold spark at the center representing certitude.
4. **The Daily Note:** A classic ruled-paper icon with a subtle gold lock shape embedded seamlessly in the margin lines, disguising the vault as a simple notepad.
5. **The Bengali 'Pa' (প) Ribbon:** An elegant, folded ribbon forming the Bengali letter "Pa", looking like a sophisticated bookmark for a personal diary.
6. **The Ink Drop:** An elegant fountain pen nib dropping a Teal ink spot (representing forensic certitude), disguising the app as a creative writing or journaling tool.
7. **Origami 'P':** A folded paper geometric design that looks like an innocent crafting, mindfulness, or note-taking app.

## Category 2: Toggled Stealth Persona (Math & Utility)
*These are activated via the app's settings. The app icon changes entirely to masquerade as a functional calculator or boring system utility.*

8. **The Equation Icon:** A simple, abstract plus/equals (`+ / =`) sign using the brand's Amethyst and Gold palette, looking purely like a modern math tool.
9. **The Golden Ratio:** A minimalist geometric spiral that hints at mathematics and calculators, while philosophically representing structure and truth.
10. **The Abacus Core:** Abstract, modern abacus beads forming a subtle 'P' pattern, hiding the app's true intent behind a math interface.
11. **Geometric Calculator 'P':** The letter P constructed entirely out of flat calculator button shapes (rounded rectangles and squares).
12. **The Sigma Shield:** A Greek sigma (∑) symbol combined with a soft, rounded shield shape in Deep Amethyst.
13. **The Forensic Lens:** Overlapping Teal and Gold circles forming a camera lens, representing forensic evidence collection, but looking like a generic camera or gallery utility app.

## Category 3: Epistemology & Certitude (Core Theme)
*These focus purely on the Bengali meaning of Protiti: "Deep Conviction" and "Undisputed Certitude."*

14. **The Spark of Realization:** A Warm Gold starburst breaking through a Deep Amethyst cloud or shield, representing the moment doubt turns to certitude.
15. **The True North:** A minimalist compass needle pointing up and right, looking like a navigation app but representing objective truth.
16. **The Cryptographic Imprint:** A stylized digital fingerprint or hash-pattern (nodding to the SHA-256 evidence sealing) that looks like abstract minimalist art.
17. **The Abstract Scale:** A highly stylized, modern geometric scale of justice that is abstracted so deeply it just looks like a 'T' and a 'P' intersecting.

## Category 4: Cultural Dignity & Growth
*These incorporate Bangladeshi cultural elements while maintaining the stealth and dignity of the app.*

18. **The Geometric Lotus:** A beautiful, calming water lily (national flower of Bangladesh) acting as a wellness app disguise, with a subtle secure shield at the center.
19. **The Woven Motif:** A traditional Bengali Jamdani geometric pattern that subtly shapes a secure vault or box.
20. **Sunrise over the Delta:** A Warm Gold semi-circle rising over a Teal line, representing hope and a new dawn of digital justice.
